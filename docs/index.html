City Tech AI Talent Pool

Faculty with a publication record in artificial intelligence, ranked by evidence. Every entry links to the papers behind it, and papers that can't be tied to City Tech for certain are marked so a person can check them.

How this list is built

Sources

Publications come from OpenAlex, which indexes Crossref, PubMed, arXiv and university repositories. The monthly update also checks ORCID records, Crossref and CUNY Academic Works. Faculty come from the City Tech department directories.

What counts as an AI paper

The paper's title uses specific AI terms (machine learning, neural network, LLM, computer vision, NLP and so on); or OpenAlex classifies it under an AI topic; or the abstract uses at least two such terms. Each paper shows the evidence that qualified it.

Verified vs. unverified

Verified papers list City Tech as this person's affiliation, or sit on their own ORCID record. Unverified papers only share an OpenAlex author profile, and for common names that profile often merges different people. Tiers count verified papers only.

Tiers

    Not on roster yet

    People who publish with a City Tech affiliation but aren't in the directories loaded so far (mostly the technology and engineering departments). They need at least four City Tech papers, with one in the last six years, to appear here. Confirm their status before relying on it.

    Limits

    Publication records miss conference talks, grants, teaching, patents and industry work. Treat this list as a starting point for outreach, not a ranking of people.
